t-SNE Manifold Properties & Tuning

How t-SNE reveals complex student micro-cohorts:

  • Local Neighborhood Preservation: Unlike linear PCA which preserves global variance, t-SNE ensures students with similar submission timelines and grades cluster together closely.
  • Perplexity Parameter: Balances attention between local micro-clusters and broad global structure. Lower values emphasize tight local niches.
  • Kullback-Leibler Divergence: Minimizes mismatch between high-dimensional Gaussian joint probabilities and low-dimensional Student-t distributions.
💡 Student Trajectory Observation: Notice the distinct separation between students who submit early coursework and those who register late with retakes.
